the issue

Topic: Domestic abuse in foster care and domestic abuse at home is still prevalent, which can result in serious health detriments in children, both mental and physical.
Question: How is the health/welfare of kids being impacted by the prevalence of abuse, at home and in foster care, and what can be done to improve it?

Child abuse is deeply correlated with understaffed & underfunded foster care. This results in greater abuse within the FC system itself and to the FC system turning a blind eye to abuse.

What is foster care? How does it differ from adoption?

  • 400,000 kids in foster care system
  • FC is a system that gives kids a safe, temporary place to stay when they can't live with their families due to abuse, neglect, or family crisis.
  • Adoption is permanent transfer of guardianship.

Children are very vulnerable

(Power To Soar)

  • almost 30% of children in foster care deal with parental substance abuse
  • 48% of foster care children are 5 yr or younger
  • up to 80% of children in FC struggle with significant mental health issues compard to 18-22% of general population.

Foster care system is understaffed

  • "[Connecticut Department of Children & Families] (DCF) data shows it has lost as many social workers as it has gained over the past five years and nearly half leave within their first two years at the agency." (CT Mirror, Tillman & Monk)
  • Since Covid, a lot of experienced social workers have left the field, creating gaps. New social workers have to work harder, leading to more burnout. (CT Mirror, Tillman & Monk).
  • Social workers earn most of their money in overtime (CT Mirror, Tillman & Monk).
  • High turnover creates gaps in a child's history & decreases accountability to the abuser & the case workers (Power To Soar)

Abuse in foster care

  • 40% of children face some form of maltreatment (Power To Soar)
  • Types of abuse: (Power to Soar)
    • 53% neglect (caregivers / caseworkers lack adequate training & resources to deal with complex trauma & behavioral issues)
    • 16% physical abuse
    • 4.4% sexual abuse
  • Abuse could lead to “PTSD, depression, and developmental delays” (Power To Soar)
  • 25% of FC children develop PTSD (Speak Up Now).
  • Foster youth exhibit high rates of debilitating depression and low self-esteem (Speak Up Now)

Benefits of Foster Care Intervention

"Children who have experienced foster care understand the necessity of their placement, even though the system is imperfect in many ways...And recent studies find that children placed in care — even if eventually reunified with their families — fare better than comparable children left at home. Crucially, they are also less likely to die or experience severe injury." (CT Mirror, Font & Hornstein)

Lack of Foster Care Intervention / Removal From Home

"Far from being overzealous, U.S. caseworkers are heavily disincentivized from placing children in foster care. Due to court and evidentiary safeguards, removing a child requires substantially more time and effort than leaving them at home. For understaffed agencies, time is hard to come by. And, once placed, the agency becomes liable for what happens to that child — but legally owes nothing to that same child if abuse continues at home." (CT Mirror, Font & Hornstein)
