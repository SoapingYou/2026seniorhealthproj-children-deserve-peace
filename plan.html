our plan for change

What will we do to alleviate social worker workload?

  1. Family Logistics: In an article from CT Mirror (Tillman & Monk), it discusses that extensive paperwork and scheduling & transporting siblings to visit each other was tiring. while directly handling paperwork may be out of the picture, we will arrange for a background-checked team to handle sibling visits and corresponding transport instead.
  2. Training: We will provide a free course made by experienced social workers from across the country on how to handle unique pressures of the job and other advice.
  3. Community: We will create a community of experienced social workers that newer social workers can always call / reach out for questions.

What will we do to spread awareness on domestic abuse?

  1. We will arrange for speakers to talk about the prevalence of domestic abuse and the importance of spotting warning signs for friends, along with pointing to resources to get help if needed
  2. We will also post a lot of social media on Instagram and Facebook targeted to both kids and adults for what is acceptable and unacceptable dynamics in a family
  3. We will ensure to understand the complex and various family dynamics of different cultures but still differentiate abusive behaviors.
