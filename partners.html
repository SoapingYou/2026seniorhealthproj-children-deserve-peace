our partner, CT DCF

Connecticut Department of Children & Families

Their Website

A report of suspected abuse/neglect is made to a state or county child protective services agency (The Up Center). In Connecticut, it's the Department of Children & Families (DCF). Their social workers are responsible for investigating the report & determining if the child is unsafe. If it is, and parents don’t address it, the social worker may petition a court to remove the child from the home.

They are our connection because not only do we strive for the same goal of keeping CT's children safe and sound within loving and supportive families, we are also directly going to partner with them.

As I mentioned before, they are understaffed and underfunded, so our nonprofit will seek to ameliorate their duties.

Their services:

  • maintains a careline (Call 1-800-842-2288) which handles behavioral issues along with teens with mental, physical health problems, and helps substance abuse within families
  • handles domestic abuse reports
  • helps reunify families & informs parents on their rights within the DCF process
  • recruits and registers foster/adoptive families
  • Has an extensive network with various resources and nonprofits like FAVOR or other care centers that help families and kids
